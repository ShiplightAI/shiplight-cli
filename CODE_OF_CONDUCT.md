# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- being welcoming to people whose experience differs from yours
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and giving it kindly
- focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it
- assuming good faith when a message reads badly — text loses tone, and many
  contributors are not writing in their first language

Behaviour that does not:

- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- harassment, public or private
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and
when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to conduct@shiplight.ai. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers will respond as they judge appropriate, up to and
including a permanent ban, and owe the reporter an account of what was done.

Maintainers who do not uphold this code in good faith may face consequences
decided by other maintainers.
